## What Is a Supplier Database?

A supplier database is a searchable directory of businesses offering goods or services, typically with:

- **Verified credentials**: CIDB grading, B-BBEE levels, CSD registration
- **Industry/category filters**: Construction, IT, logistics, manufacturing, etc.
- **Geographic reach**: Provinces, cities, service areas
- **Compliance status**: Tax clearance, CSD validity, insurance
- **Past performance**: References, completed projects, ratings

Think of it as LinkedIn for suppliers—but focused on procurement, certifications, and capacity rather than just work history.

## Common Certifications Explained

### CIDB Grading (Construction)

The Construction Industry Development Board (CIDB) rates contractors 1-9 by experience, financial capacity, and track record. Higher grades = bigger projects.

**Example**: A Grade 5 contractor can bid on tenders up to ~R50M.

### B-BBEE Level

Broad-Based Black Economic Empowerment (B-BBEE) rates companies 1-8 by black ownership, management, and procurement. Many government tenders require Level 1-4.

### CSD Registration

The Central Supplier Database (CSD) is SA's official supplier registry. All government suppliers must register here. You can verify any supplier's CSD number online.

### Tax Clearance

SARS issues tax clearance certificates to compliant suppliers. Government tenders often require current clearance.

## Best Practices for Using Supplier Databases

1. **Verify credentials independently** — Cross-check CIDB grading, CSD registration, B-BBEE level on official government websites
2. **Check references** — Contact past clients; ask for project examples
3. **Diversify suppliers** — Don't rely on one source; use multiple suppliers
4. **Update regularly** — Supplier circumstances change; revisit certifications annually
5. **Monitor performance** — Track delivery, quality, compliance after award

# B-BBEE Verification: What Buyers Look For & How to Improve Your Level

**Meta Description:** Understand B-BBEE levels and verification in SA procurement. Learn requirements, how to get certified, and why government tenders require B-BBEE Level 3+.

---

## What Is B-BBEE?

**B-BBEE** = **Broad-Based Black Economic Empowerment**

It's South Africa's policy to measure and promote black ownership, management, and participation in businesses. Government uses B-BBEE as a criteria in procurement—meaning your B-BBEE level directly impacts your ability to win tenders.

**In procurement**: Most government tenders require suppliers to be at least **B-BBEE Level 3 or better**. If you're Level 4-8, you're less competitive; if you're Level 1-2, you have a major advantage.

---

## B-BBEE Levels Explained (1-8)

| Level | Black Ownership | Typical Scoring | Government Preference |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Level 1** | 95%+ | 100 points | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Highest priority |
| **Level 2** | 75-95% | 95-100 points | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ High priority |
| **Level 3** | 50-75% | 85-95 points | ⭐⭐⭐ Competitive |
| **Level 4** | 25-50% | 75-85 points | ⭐⭐ Less preferred |
| **Level 5** | 10-25% | 65-75 points | ⭐ Low priority |
| **Level 6** | 5-10% | 55-65 points | Limited eligibility |
| **Level 7** | <5% | 45-55 points | Very limited |
| **Level 8** | Non-compliant | 0-45 points | Ineligible for most |

**Key insight**: Level 1 companies get substantial scoring advantage in government tenders. Level 3 is minimum for serious government business. Level 8 (non-compliant) = essentially shut out.

## What Goes Into Your B-BBEE Score?

B-BBEE is calculated across 5 pillars:

### 1. **Ownership (25 points)**
- Who owns the company (% black ownership)
- Economic interest (profit rights)
- Management rights

**Example**: 100% black-owned = 25/25 points. 50% black-owned = 12/25 points.

### 2. **Management (10 points)**
- Board composition (% black directors)
- Executive management (% black C-suite)

**Example**: 50% black board = 5/10 points.

### 3. **Employment Equity (10 points)**
- Black staff at all levels
- Women in management

**Example**: 40% black workforce, 20% women in management = 8/10 points.

### 4. **Procurement (15 points)**
- % of procurement from B-BBEE suppliers
- Spending on black-owned businesses

**Example**: 40% procurement from black suppliers = 12/15 points.

### 5. **Skills Development (10 points)**
- Investment in training & development
- Bursaries for black youth

**Example**: 2% payroll spent on training = 8/10 points.

**Enterprise Development (5 bonus points)**:
- Support for black-owned businesses
- Mentorship, financing, etc.

**Socio-Economic Development (5 bonus points)**:
- Community projects
- CSR activities

**Total possible**: 100 points → **Level 1 (90+ points)**

## How to Get Your B-BBEE Certificate

### Step 1: Find a B-BBEE Verification Agent

B-BBEE certificates are issued by **SANAS-accredited** (South African National Accreditation System) companies. You cannot self-certify.

**Options**:
- Large accounting firms (Deloitte, KPMG, PwC, Grant Thornton)
- Smaller B-BBEE specialists (more affordable)
- Search: "SANAS B-BBEE Verification Agent" + your province

### Step 2: Gather Required Documents

**Financial**:
- Last 2-3 years audited or reviewed financial statements
- Tax compliance (SARS)
- Shareholder register (proving ownership %)

**Operational**:
- Employment records (payroll, staff list with race/gender)
- Procurement records (supplier list showing B-BBEE status)
- Training records (investment in skills development)
- Board composition details

**Administrative**:
- Company registration (CIPC)
- Shareholder agreements
- Employment equity plan

### Step 3: Verification Agent Conducts Assessment

The agent will:
- Review all documents
- Score your company across 5 pillars
- Calculate your final B-BBEE level
- Issue a certificate if you qualify

**Timeline**: 3-6 weeks  
**Cost**: R8,000 - R25,000 depending on company size and agent

### Step 4: Receive Your B-BBEE Certificate

You'll receive:
- **Certificate document** (PDF/physical)
- **Certificate number** (for reference on tenders)
- **Validity period** (typically 3 years)
- **Scorecard** (breakdown by pillar)

## Common B-BBEE Issues & Solutions

### Issue: You're Level 5-8 and Want to Improve

**To move from Level 5 → Level 3**:

1. **Increase black ownership** (biggest impact)
   - Bring in black investor/partner
   - Target: Move from 10% → 50% black ownership

2. **Improve procurement**
   - Source 30%+ from B-BBEE suppliers instead of white-owned
   - Quick win: 3-5 points improvement

3. **Grow black management**
   - Hire/promote black managers & directors
   - Target: 50%+ black board

**Timeline**: Can move up 1-2 levels in 1-2 years with focus.

### Issue: You're Non-Compliant (Level 8)

**Reasons**:
- Not registered with SARS
- Tax debt/default
- Fraudulent ownership
- No compliance with employment law

**Solution**: Fix compliance issues first (tax, registration), then re-apply for B-BBEE verification.

### Issue: Your Certificate Expired

**Action**: Re-apply for new verification. You cannot bid on government tenders with expired B-BBEE.

## B-BBEE in Government Procurement

### How Tenders Use B-BBEE

**Evaluation criteria**: Most government RFQs include B-BBEE as scoring:

Example tender scoring:
- **Price**: 60 points
- **B-BBEE Level**: 20 points
- **Experience/Quality**: 20 points

**B-BBEE points awarded**:
- Level 1: 20/20 points
- Level 2: 18/20 points
- Level 3: 15/20 points
- Level 4: 12/20 points
- Level 5+: 8/20 points

**Impact**: Level 1 supplier needs 5% lower price to match Level 5 supplier on total score.

### Procurement Targets

Government departments have **B-BBEE procurement targets**:
- **30% of spend** from Level 1-2 suppliers
- **20% of spend** from Level 3-4 suppliers
- **Emphasis on women-owned, youth-owned, disability-focused**

This means there's genuine demand for qualified B-BBEE suppliers.

## Maintaining Your B-BBEE Level

### Annual Compliance Checklist

- ✓ Pay all taxes on time (SARS compliance)
- ✓ Maintain employment equity (black staff %)
- ✓ Track procurement from B-BBEE suppliers
- ✓ Record skills development investments
- ✓ Board composition remains aligned

### Preparing for Re-Certification (Every 3 Years)

**6 months before expiry**:
1. Review your current scorecard
2. Identify improvement opportunities
3. Document compliance across all pillars
4. Contact your verification agent
5. Submit for re-certification

### Medium-Term: Ownership (10+ points)

## B-BBEE + Other Certifications

You typically need **all four**:
1. **B-BBEE certificate** (empowerment)
2. **CIDB grading** (if construction)
3. **CSD registration** (supplier database)
4. **Tax clearance** (SARS compliance)

Without all four, you're ineligible for most government tenders.

# How to Register on CSD: Step-by-Step Guide for South African Suppliers

**Meta Description:** Register your business on the Central Supplier Database (CSD) in minutes. Step-by-step guide with required documents, timelines, and government tender eligibility.

---

## Introduction

If your business supplies goods or services to South African government departments, you need to be registered on the **Central Supplier Database (CSD)**.

The CSD is the government's official supplier registry. Without a CSD registration, you cannot bid on government tenders—no matter how qualified you are.

This guide walks you through the entire registration process, required documents, timelines, and common mistakes to avoid.

---

## What Is CSD Registration?

The **Central Supplier Database (CSD)** is South Africa's official supplier registry maintained by the National Treasury. It's required for:

- **All government procurement** — From national departments to municipalities
- **Proof of supplier legitimacy** — Government validates your business exists and is compliant
- **Tender eligibility** — Most government RFQs require an active CSD registration
- **Performance tracking** — Government records your contract history

**Key fact**: CSD registration is **free**. There's no fee to register or maintain your record.

---

## Who Needs CSD Registration?

You need CSD registration if:

✓ Your business supplies to government (national, provincial, municipal, public entities)  
✓ You want to bid on government tenders  
✓ You're a contractor, service provider, or goods supplier  
✓ You're a B-BBEE supplier seeking government work  

You do NOT need CSD if:
- You only sell to private companies
- You don't plan to bid on government tenders

---

## Benefits of CSD Registration

1. **Tender eligibility** — Qualify for government RFQs and tenders
2. **Credibility** — Government suppliers view your registration as proof of legitimacy
3. **Visibility** — Buyers can find you when searching the supplier database
4. **Performance tracking** — Build a track record of completed contracts
5. **No cost** — Registration and maintenance are free

## Step 1: Prepare Required Documents

Before registering, gather these documents:

### For Individual Proprietors:
- Copy of ID (front & back)
- Proof of residence (utility bill, lease agreement, bank statement from last 3 months)
- Business registration document (if registered)
- Tax number (SARS PIN)

### For Companies (CC, Pty Ltd, etc.):
- Copy of company registration certificate (CIPC)
- Copy of directors' IDs (front & back)
- Proof of business address (utility bill, lease, municipal rates document)
- SARS Tax Number
- Bank account details (for payments, if awarded contracts)

### For Trusts & Close Corporations:
- Trust deed or CC registration certificate
- Trustees/members' ID copies
- Proof of business address
- SARS Tax Number

### Additional (If Applicable):
- CIDB grading certificate (if construction)
- B-BBEE certificate (if claiming B-BBEE status)
- CSD number (if already registered — for updates)

**Tip**: Scan all documents in PDF format before starting the online registration.

---

## Step 2: Access the CSD Registration Portal

1. **Go to**: https://www.csd.gov.za
2. **Click**: "Register as a New Supplier" or "Supplier Registration"
3. **Read**: The CSD Code of Conduct (you'll need to agree)
4. **Create**: Login credentials (email and password)

---

## Step 3: Complete the Online Registration Form

### Section 1: Business Information
- **Business Name**: Exact name as registered with CIPC or tax authority
- **Business Type**: Select (Sole Proprietor, CC, Pty Ltd, Trust, etc.)
- **SARS Tax Number**: Your 10-digit tax reference number
- **Business Address**: Physical location (not PO Box)
- **Email**: Active email for CSD notifications
- **Phone**: Primary contact number

### Section 2: Banking Details
- **Account Holder**: Name matching business registration
- **Bank Name**: (ABSA, FNB, Standard Bank, etc.)
- **Account Number**: For receiving payments if awarded
- **Branch Code**: 6-digit code (ask your bank)

### Section 3: Ownership & Management
- **Owner/Directors**: Full names, ID numbers
- **Percentage Ownership**: For multi-owner businesses
- **B-BBEE Status**: Declare if you're B-BBEE Level 1-8

### Section 4: Business Services
- **Industry**: Select your primary category (Construction, IT, Cleaning, Transport, etc.)
- **Services/Products**: Describe what you supply
- **Geographic Areas**: Provinces where you operate
- **Capacity**: Estimated annual turnover (optional but helpful)

### Section 5: Compliance
- **Tax Status**: Declare if compliant with SARS
- **Audit/Financial Statements**: Upload if you have them
- **References**: Optional — supplier contact details

---

## Step 4: Upload Required Documents

The CSD portal will ask for:

1. **ID Copy** (front & back)
2. **Proof of Address** (utility bill, lease, municipal rates — no older than 3 months)
3. **Company Registration** (CIPC certificate or business registration)
4. **Banking Details Confirmation** (bank letter or screenshot)

**File Requirements**:
- PDF or JPG format
- Maximum 5MB per file
- Clear, legible scans (no blurry or cut-off images)

**Common mistakes**:
- ❌ Outdated proof of address (older than 3 months)
- ❌ Blurry scans that are hard to read
- ❌ PO Box instead of physical address
- ❌ Missing director/owner ID

---

## Step 5: Review & Submit

1. **Review** all information for accuracy
2. **Verify** that documents are uploaded and legible
3. **Agree** to the CSD Code of Conduct
4. **Submit** your registration

**Status**: You'll receive a confirmation email within 24 hours.

---

## Step 6: Wait for Verification (3-5 Business Days)

After submission, CSD will:

1. **Verify** your business exists and is legitimate
2. **Check** your tax status with SARS
3. **Validate** your documents
4. **Contact you** if there are issues

**Timeline**: 
- **Fast approval**: 3 business days (if everything is correct)
- **Delayed approval**: 5-10 business days (if they need clarification)
- **Rejection**: Rare, but happens if documents are fake or incomplete

**If rejected**, you'll receive an email explaining why. You can reapply after fixing the issues.

---

## Step 7: Receive Your CSD Number

Once approved, you'll receive:

- **CSD Number**: A unique 10-digit ID for your business
- **Registration Certificate**: PDF download (print and keep)
- **Login Access**: To update your profile anytime

**Your CSD number**: This is what government buyers use to verify your registration when you bid on tenders.

---

## How to Update Your CSD Registration

After registration, you can update your profile anytime:

1. **Log in** to https://www.csd.gov.za with your credentials
2. **Click** "Update Profile" or "Manage Registration"
3. **Change**: Contact details, services offered, banking info, etc.
4. **Submit**: Changes are usually updated within 24 hours

**When to update**:
- New phone number or email
- Change of business address
- New services offered
- Updated B-BBEE certificate
- Banking details change

---

## CSD Verification: Check If a Supplier Is Registered

**As a buyer**, you can verify any supplier's CSD status:

1. Go to https://www.csd.gov.za
2. Click "Supplier Search" or "Verify Supplier"
3. Enter supplier name or CSD number
4. View: Registration date, address, contact info, B-BBEE status

This is how government buyers confirm suppliers are legitimate.

## Common Questions About CSD Registration

### Q: How much does CSD registration cost?
**A**: It's free. No registration fees, no annual fees, no maintenance costs.

### Q: How long does approval take?
**A**: 3-5 business days if documents are complete. Longer if CSD needs clarification.

### Q: Can I register multiple businesses?
**A**: Yes, each business needs its own CSD registration with a separate email address.

### Q: Does CSD registration expire?
**A**: No, but you must keep your information current. Update if your address or services change.

### Q: Can I delete my CSD registration?
**A**: Yes, contact CSD support. But you won't be able to bid on government tenders after deletion.

### Q: What if my CSD number is rejected on a tender?
**A**: It may be expired, inactive, or there's a mismatch. Log in to CSD and verify your status. Contact CSD support if issues persist.

### Q: Can I use a PO Box for my CSD address?
**A**: No. CSD requires a physical business address.

### Q: Do I need a separate CSD for close corporations?
**A**: Yes, each CC is registered separately. If you have a CC and a Pty Ltd, they need separate registrations.

## Troubleshooting: What If Registration Is Delayed?

**Issue**: "Approval pending" after 5+ days

**Solutions**:
1. Check your email (including spam folder) — CSD may have contacted you
2. Log in to CSD and check your registration status
3. Contact CSD support: support@csd.gov.za
4. Resubmit documents if CSD requested clarification

**Issue**: "Registration rejected"

**Common reasons**:
- Proof of address is outdated (>3 months old)
- Documents are blurry or incomplete
- Mismatch between business name and registration
- Invalid SARS tax number

**Solution**: Review CSD's rejection email, fix the issue, and reapply.

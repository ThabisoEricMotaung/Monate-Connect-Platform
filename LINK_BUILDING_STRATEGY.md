## Tips for Success

1. **Personalization is key:** Mention specific articles/pages you've read on their site
2. **Lead with value:** Show how linking helps *their* audience, not just you
3. **Use existing content:** Link to published articles (RFQ guide, B-BBEE guide, government tenders article)
4. **Follow up:** 80% of links come from 2nd or 3rd follow-up, not the first email
5. **Track everything:** Use a spreadsheet to monitor outreach, responses, and link publication
6. **Reciprocate:** Offer links back where genuine (don't do link exchanges with low-authority sites)

# CIDB Grading: Contractor Grades 1-9 Explained — How to Apply & Benefits

**Meta Description:** Understand CIDB grading (1-9) for construction contractors in SA. Learn requirements, how to apply, costs, and bid limits for each grade.

## What Is CIDB Grading?

**CIDB** stands for **Construction Industry Development Board**. It's a South African statutory body that grades construction contractors based on:

- **Financial capacity** — How much money the company has
- **Experience** — Years in business and completed projects
- **Technical competence** — Qualifications and track record
- **Legal compliance** — Tax status, insurance, registrations

CIDB assigns grades **1-9**, where:
- **Grade 1** = Smallest capacity (projects up to ~R500K)
- **Grade 9** = Largest capacity (projects over R1B+)

**Why it matters**: Government and private tenders for construction work often specify a minimum CIDB grade. You can't bid on a tender unless you meet the grade requirement.

## CIDB Grade Requirements

To qualify for each grade, you need:

### Financial Capacity
- **Grade 1-3**: R100K-R500K in net assets
- **Grade 4-5**: R1M-R10M in net assets
- **Grade 6-7**: R20M-R100M in net assets
- **Grade 8-9**: R100M+ in net assets

Proven by:
- Latest audited financial statements
- Bank statements showing cash reserves
- Accountant's letter confirming net assets

### Experience
- **Grade 1**: 1 year in construction
- **Grade 2-3**: 2-3 years, with completed projects as reference
- **Grade 4-5**: 5-10 years, with major projects completed
- **Grade 6+**: 15+ years with significant project history

Proven by:
- Company registration & operating history
- References from past clients
- Photos/documentation of completed projects
- Contracts with contract values matching claimed grade

### Technical Competence
- **Directors/managers** must have construction experience or qualifications
- **Staff** must include qualified supervisors/technicians
- **Plant & equipment** appropriate for the grade
- **Track record** of delivering quality projects on time

### Legal Compliance
- ✓ Tax registration (SARS PIN)
- ✓ Workers' compensation insurance (if applicable)
- ✓ Public liability insurance (if applicable)
- ✓ No legal disputes or contract defaults
- ✓ Legitimate business registration (CIPC, DTI)

## How to Apply for CIDB Grading

### Step 1: Prepare Your Application Dossier

Gather:
- **Company registration** (CIPC certificate)
- **Financial statements** (last 2-3 years, audited if applicable)
- **Bank statements** (last 6 months showing liquidity)
- **Project references** (contracts, completion certificates, client letters)
- **Insurance certificates** (public liability, workers' compensation)
- **CVs** of directors and key staff
- **Photos** of past projects (if available)
- **Tax compliance certificate** (SARS)

### Step 2: Register on CIDB Online System

1. Go to https://www.cidb.org.za
2. Click "Register as a Contractor"
3. Create login credentials
4. Complete contractor profile (business info, contact details, services)
5. Upload all supporting documents

### Step 3: Submit Your Grading Application

1. In your CIDB profile, select "Apply for Grading"
2. Choose your desired grade (be realistic based on financials & experience)
3. Upload all required documents
4. Pay application fee (approximately R2,500-R5,000 depending on grade)
5. Submit

### Step 4: CIDB Assessment (4-8 Weeks)

CIDB will:
- Review your financial statements
- Verify your project references
- Check your company's legal standing
- Assess your track record

**Possible outcomes**:
- ✓ **Approved** — You get your grading certificate (valid 2 years)
- ⚠️ **Conditional** — Approved with conditions (e.g., "approved if financials improve")
- ❌ **Rejected** — Does not meet requirements for your requested grade

**If rejected**: You can reapply at a lower grade, or address the issues and reapply later.

### Step 5: Receive Your Grading Certificate

Once approved, CIDB issues:
- **Grading Certificate** (PDF or physical)
- **Certificate Number** (for reference on tenders)
- **Validity Period** (usually 2 years, then renew)

You can download your certificate anytime from your CIDB profile.

## CIDB Grade Maintenance & Renewal

### Annual Compliance
- Keep your **financial statements** current
- Maintain **insurance** (public liability, workers' comp)
- Stay **tax compliant** with SARS
- Avoid legal disputes / contract defaults

### Certificate Renewal (Every 2 Years)

Before your certificate expires:
1. Log into CIDB
2. Click "Renew Grading"
3. Submit updated financials, insurance, project updates
4. Pay renewal fee (~R1,500-R3,000)
5. CIDB reviews and reissues certificate

**Why renew?** Tenders require a **current** grading certificate. Expired grades = ineligible to bid.

## Common Questions About CIDB Grading

### Q: Can I start with Grade 1 and upgrade later?
**A**: Yes. Start at Grade 1, build experience, then apply for Grade 2-3 later. Most contractors upgrade every 2-3 years as they grow.

### Q: What if I don't have audited financials?
**A**: You'll need an accountant to prepare financials. Costs ~R5K-R15K. Non-negotiable for CIDB.

### Q: Can I bid on tenders above my grade?
**A**: No. If a tender requires Grade 5 and you're Grade 3, you're ineligible. You can only bid on tenders matching or below your grade.

### Q: What if my grading is rejected?
**A**: Review the rejection reason. Common issues: insufficient financials, weak project references, or no experience. Address these and reapply at a lower grade.

### Q: How often do I need to renew?
**A**: Every 2 years. Mark your calendar so you don't let it expire.

### Q: Can I appeal a rejection?
**A**: Yes. Contact CIDB and request to appeal. Provide additional evidence of experience or financials.

### Q: Does CIDB check my tax status?
**A**: Yes. CIDB verifies you're compliant with SARS before issuing grading.

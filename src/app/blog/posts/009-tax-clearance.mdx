# SARS Tax Clearance Certificate: Requirements & How to Verify

A **tax clearance certificate** from SARS is required for government suppliers. Without it, you're ineligible for most tenders.

## What Is a Tax Clearance Certificate?

A tax clearance certificate is SARS's confirmation that:
- You've registered for tax
- You're compliant with tax obligations
- You have no outstanding tax debt

**Valid for**: 12 months from issue date

## Who Needs It

✓ All government suppliers  
✓ Tender bidders  
✓ Construction contractors  
✓ Professional service providers

## How to Apply

1. Register with SARS (if not already registered)
2. Log in to SARS e-Services
3. Go to "Tax Compliance Status" section
4. Request tax clearance certificate
5. Pay nominal fee (usually R0, sometimes R50)
6. Certificate issued immediately or within 24 hours

## Required Documents

- Business registration (CIPC certificate)
- Tax number (SARS PIN)
- Valid ID (for directors/sole proprietor)
- Proof of address

## Timeline

- **Online**: Issued immediately
- **By post**: 3-5 working days

## How to Verify a Supplier's Tax Status

1. Go to SARS website
2. Use "Verify Tax Status" tool
3. Enter supplier name or tax number
4. View their tax compliance status

## Cost

**Usually free.** SARS may charge R50 for urgent processing (optional).

## Expiration & Renewal

- **Valid for**: 12 months
- **Renew**: Before expiration by logging into SARS e-Services
- **Lapsed**: If expired, you cannot bid on government tenders

## Common Issues

**Non-compliant status**: Usually because:
- Outstanding tax debt
- Non-filer (didn't submit returns)
- SARS dispute

**Solution**: Contact SARS, resolve outstanding issues, reapply.

## Checklist

- [ ] Register with SARS
- [ ] Get SARS PIN
- [ ] Log into SARS e-Services
- [ ] Request tax clearance
- [ ] Download/print certificate
- [ ] Add to tender submissions
- [ ] Set 12-month reminder to renew
- [ ] Update supplier profiles with status

---

**Don't let tax clearance expire.** Set a calendar reminder to renew before deadline.

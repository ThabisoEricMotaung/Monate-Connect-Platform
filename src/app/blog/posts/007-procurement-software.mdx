# Best Procurement Software for SMEs in South Africa

SMEs struggle with manual procurement: spreadsheets, email chains, lost deadlines, duplicate suppliers.

Procurement software solves this by:
- **Centralizing RFQs** — One platform for all supplier requests
- **Automating workflow** — RFQ → Response → Comparison → Award
- **Supplier management** — One database of all suppliers
- **Tender tracking** — Never miss a deadline
- **Cost visibility** — See spending by category, supplier

## What to Look For

1. **RFQ Management** — Create, distribute, compare quotes
2. **Supplier Database** — Store, filter, verify suppliers
3. **Tender Tracking** — Monitor government tenders matching your criteria
4. **Compliance Tracking** — CIDB, B-BBEE, CSD, tax clearance
5. **Integration** — Works with QuickBooks, Xero, accounting software
6. **Ease of use** — No IT background needed
7. **Price** — Affordable for SMEs (R500-5K/month)

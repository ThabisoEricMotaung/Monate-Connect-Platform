# Supplier Verification Checklist: CIDB, B-BBEE, CSD, Tax Clearance

Before awarding a contract, verify every supplier meets compliance requirements.

## The 4 Key Verifications

### 1. CSD Registration (Central Supplier Database)

**What**: Official supplier registry  
**Where**: https://www.csd.gov.za  
**How to verify**:
1. Go to CSD Supplier Search
2. Enter supplier name or CSD number
3. Confirm: Active status, correct address, contact details

**What to look for**: Active status (not inactive or expired)

### 2. B-BBEE Level

**What**: Black economic empowerment certification  
**Where**: Request certificate from supplier or SANAS accreditor  
**Valid for**: 3 years

**What to check**:
- Certificate not expired
- Issued by SANAS-accredited verifier
- Level matches tender requirement (minimum Level 3 for most gov tenders)

### 3. CIDB Grading (Construction Only)

**What**: Contractor capacity & experience rating  
**Where**: https://www.cidb.org.za → Contractor Search  
**How to verify**:
1. Go to CIDB website
2. Enter contractor name or grade certificate number
3. Confirm: Grade level, validity date, contact details

**What to check**:
- Current grade (not expired)
- Grade meets tender requirement (e.g., Grade 5+ for large projects)
- No disputes or defaults listed

### 4. SARS Tax Clearance

**What**: Tax compliance verification  
**Where**: SARS e-Services or request from supplier  
**Valid for**: 12 months

**How to verify**:
1. Request certificate from supplier
2. Verify it's not expired
3. Check supplier name matches exactly

## Red Flags

🚩 **Expired certifications** — Disqualifying  
🚩 **Name mismatch** — Different company name on cert  
🚩 **CIDB disputes** — Unresolved legal issues  
🚩 **No valid contact** — Can't reach supplier  
🚩 **Multiple rejections** — History of non-performance

## Verification Tools (Free)

- **CSD**: https://www.csd.gov.za (Supplier Search)
- **CIDB**: https://www.cidb.org.za (Contractor Search)
- **SARS**: SARS e-Services (Tax Compliance Status)
- **B-BBEE**: Request from supplier (no public database)

## When to Re-Verify

- **CSD**: Annually
- **B-BBEE**: Every 3 years (or if supplier claims upgrade)
- **CIDB**: Every 2 years (renewal cycle)
- **Tax Clearance**: Every 12 months (expires)

## Full Verification Workflow

1. **Initial verification** (before RFQ)
   - Check CSD, CIDB, B-BBEE, tax clearance
   - Confirm: eligible to bid

2. **Bid evaluation**
   - Re-verify certifications valid
   - Check no new disputes/issues

3. **Award stage**
   - Final verification all certs current
   - Confirm supplier can start work

4. **Ongoing** (quarterly or annually)
   - Spot-check certifications
   - Ensure continued compliance

---

**Don't skip verification.** Award to non-compliant suppliers = contractual risk, government audit findings, payment delays.

**Use this checklist for every supplier.**

# How to Register on CSD: Step-by-Step Guide

If your business supplies goods or services to South African government departments, you need to be registered on the **Central Supplier Database (CSD)**.

The CSD is the government's official supplier registry. Without a CSD registration, you cannot bid on government tenders—no matter how qualified you are.

## What Is CSD?

The **Central Supplier Database (CSD)** is South Africa's official supplier registry maintained by the National Treasury. It's required for all government procurement, proof of supplier legitimacy, and tender eligibility.

**Key fact**: CSD registration is **free**. No fees to register or maintain your record.

## Who Needs CSD Registration?

You need CSD registration if:
- Your business supplies to government (national, provincial, municipal)
- You want to bid on government tenders
- You're a contractor, service provider, or goods supplier

## Benefits

1. **Tender eligibility** — Qualify for government RFQs and tenders
2. **Credibility** — Government suppliers view your registration as proof of legitimacy
3. **Visibility** — Buyers can find you when searching the supplier database
4. **Performance tracking** — Build a track record of completed contracts
5. **No cost** — Registration and maintenance are free

## Step 1: Prepare Required Documents

**For Individual Proprietors**:
- Copy of ID (front & back)
- Proof of residence (utility bill, lease, bank statement <3 months)
- Business registration document
- Tax number (SARS PIN)

**For Companies**:
- Company registration certificate (CIPC)
- Directors' IDs (front & back)
- Proof of business address
- SARS Tax Number
- Bank account details

## Step 2-6: Registration Process

1. Go to https://www.csd.gov.za
2. Click "Register as a New Supplier"
3. Complete online form with business info, banking details, ownership, services
4. Upload required documents (ID, proof of address, company registration)
5. Review and submit
6. Wait 3-5 business days for verification and approval

## Timeline & Cost

- **Processing time**: 3-5 business days for fast approval, up to 10 days if clarification needed
- **Cost**: FREE

## CSD Verification

As a buyer, verify any supplier's CSD status:
1. Go to https://www.csd.gov.za
2. Click "Supplier Search"
3. Enter supplier name or CSD number
4. View registration date, address, contact info

## Common Questions

**Q: How much does CSD registration cost?**
A: It's free. No registration fees, no annual fees.

**Q: How long does approval take?**
A: 3-5 business days if documents are complete.

**Q: Does CSD registration expire?**
A: No, but you must keep your information current.

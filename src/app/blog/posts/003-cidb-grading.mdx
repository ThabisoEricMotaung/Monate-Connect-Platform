# CIDB Grading: Contractor Grades 1-9 Explained

**CIDB** stands for **Construction Industry Development Board**. It's a South African statutory body that grades construction contractors based on financial capacity, experience, technical competence, and legal compliance.

CIDB assigns grades **1-9**, where Grade 1 = smallest capacity (up to R500K projects) and Grade 9 = largest capacity (R1B+ projects).

## Why It Matters

Government and private tenders for construction often specify a minimum CIDB grade. You can't bid on a tender unless you meet the grade requirement.

## CIDB Grades 1-9 Breakdown

| Grade | Max Project Value | Experience | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ~R500K | 1-2 years | Small residential |
| 2 | ~R1M | 2-3 years | House extensions |
| 3 | ~R2M | 3-5 years | Medium commercial |
| 4 | ~R5M | 5+ years | Larger commercial |
| 5 | ~R50M | 10+ years | Major commercial |
| 6 | ~R150M | 15+ years | Large infrastructure |
| 7 | ~R500M | 20+ years | Major infrastructure |
| 8+ | R1B+ | 25+ years | National/mega projects |

## Requirements by Grade

**Financial Capacity**:
- Grade 1-3: R100K-R500K net assets
- Grade 4-5: R1M-R10M net assets
- Grade 6+: R20M+ net assets

**Experience**:
- Grade 1: 1 year in construction
- Grade 2-3: 2-3 years with completed projects
- Grade 4-5: 5-10 years, major projects
- Grade 6+: 15+ years with significant history

**Technical**: Directors/managers must have construction experience; staff qualifications; appropriate equipment

**Legal Compliance**: Tax registration, workers' comp insurance, public liability, no legal disputes

## How to Apply for CIDB Grading

1. **Prepare documents**:
   - Company registration (CIPC)
   - 2-3 years audited financials
   - Bank statements (6 months)
   - 3-5 project references with photos
   - Insurance certificates
   - SARS tax compliance
   - Director CVs

2. **Register on CIDB.org.za** and create profile

3. **Submit grading application** with documents and R2,500-R5,000 fee

4. **CIDB assessment** (4-8 weeks):
   - Review financials
   - Verify project references
   - Check legal standing
   - Assess track record

5. **Receive certificate** (valid 2 years, then renew)

## Timeline & Cost

- **Preparation**: 1-2 weeks
- **Review**: 4-8 weeks
- **Total**: 6-12 weeks
- **Cost**: R10K-R20K (includes accountant fees, application fee)

## Maintenance & Renewal

### Annual Compliance
- Keep financials current
- Maintain insurance
- Stay tax compliant
- Avoid legal disputes

### Renewal (Every 2 Years)
Before certificate expires:
1. Log into CIDB
2. Submit updated financials, insurance, projects
3. Pay renewal fee (~R1,500-R3,000)
4. CIDB reissues certificate

**Critical**: Expired grades = ineligible to bid. Don't let it lapse.

## Benefits of CIDB Grading

1. Bid on government & private tenders
2. Credibility with buyers
3. Access to bigger projects (higher grade)
4. Loan access (banks trust CIDB)
5. Insurance discounts

## Common Questions

**Q: Can I start Grade 1 and upgrade later?**  
A: Yes. Build experience, then apply for Grade 2-3. Most contractors upgrade every 2-3 years.

**Q: What if I don't have audited financials?**  
A: Hire an accountant (R5K-R15K). Non-negotiable for CIDB.

**Q: Can I bid above my grade?**  
A: No. Grade 3 can only bid on tenders for Grade 3 or below.

**Q: What if rejected?**  
A: Review reasons, address issues, reapply at lower grade.

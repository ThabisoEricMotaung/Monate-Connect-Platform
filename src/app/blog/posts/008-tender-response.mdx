# How to Write a Winning Tender Response: 10 Expert Tips

80% of tender rejections happen because proposals fail to follow instructions—not because of price or qualifications.

Here's how to avoid that.

## 10 Tips for Winning Tenders

### 1. Read the RFQ Thoroughly (Twice)
- Read once for understanding
- Read again for detail
- Highlight all requirements
- Flag deadlines, submission method, format requirements

### 2. Respond to EVERY Requirement
- If RFQ asks 20 questions, answer 20
- Use point-by-point format: "Requirement → Your response"
- Don't assume they'll infer; state it explicitly

### 3. Use the Tender Template Provided
- RFQs usually have a template
- Follow it exactly
- Don't reformat or "improve" it
- Buyers use automated systems to score responses

### 4. Address Evaluation Criteria in Your Bid
- RFQ lists how you'll be scored (price, experience, B-BBEE, etc.)
- Structure your response around those criteria
- Emphasize where you score highest

### 5. Include Evidence
- Don't just claim experience—prove it
- Provide references (3-5 past clients with contact details)
- Include photos of past projects
- Attach completion certificates

### 6. Price Competitively but Sustainably
- Price too high → you lose
- Price too low → buyer questions quality or you go bankrupt
- Research market rates
- Show your cost breakdown (not always required, but builds confidence)

### 7. Proofread & Check Compliance
- Typos look unprofessional
- Grammar errors suggest lack of attention
- Verify all numbers (pricing, dates, specs)
- Ensure all required documents are attached

### 8. Submit Early
- Don't submit 1 minute before deadline
- Technical glitches happen
- Early submission shows professionalism
- Best: submit 24-48 hours early

### 9. Follow Submission Instructions Exactly
- If they say "PDF", don't send Word
- If they say "email to X", don't use online portal
- If they want "no more than 10 pages", don't send 11
- Wrong format = automatic rejection

### 10. Get a Second Opinion
- Have colleague review before submitting
- Fresh eyes catch errors you missed
- Check that claims are believable

## Common Rejection Reasons

1. **Missed deadline** (25% of rejections)
2. **Missing required document** (20%)
3. **Didn't answer all questions** (15%)
4. **Non-compliant format** (10%)
5. **Uncompetitive pricing** (10%)
6. **Lack of evidence/references** (10%)
7. **Quality/professionalism concerns** (10%)

## Pre-Submission Checklist

- [ ] Read RFQ twice, highlighted all requirements
- [ ] Answered every question
- [ ] Used provided template
- [ ] Addressed all evaluation criteria
- [ ] Included 3+ references with contact details
- [ ] Added photos/evidence of past work
- [ ] Proofread for typos & grammar
- [ ] Verified all pricing & numbers
- [ ] Checked all required documents are attached
- [ ] Verified format requirements met
- [ ] Submitted 24+ hours before deadline
- [ ] Got second opinion

## Winning Bid Structure

**1. Executive Summary** (1 page)
- Who you are
- Why you're qualified
- Key strengths
- Value proposition

**2. Experience** (2-3 pages)
- Relevant past projects
- References
- Certifications (CIDB, B-BBEE, CSD)

**3. Approach** (2-3 pages)
- How you'll deliver
- Timeline
- Team/resources

**4. Pricing** (1 page)
- Total price
- Cost breakdown (if requested)

**5. Compliance** (1 page)
- Tax clearance
- Insurance
- B-BBEE certificate
- CIDB grading

# Tender Alerts: Get Notified of RFQs Instantly

## The Problem: Missing Tenders Before Deadline

You've heard this:

*"There was a R500K tender in our category last month, but we found out after it closed."*

This happens because:

1. **Tenders on multiple platforms** — eTenders, department websites, municipalities. No single source.
2. **Tight deadlines** — 2-3 weeks to submit. Manual searching means you miss opportunities.
3. **Easy to miss** — Announced once on government sites, not publicized widely.

**Result**: Massive lost revenue. Competitors beat you because they saw first.

## The Solution: Tender Alerts

Tender alerts automatically:

- **Scan government platforms daily** for tenders matching your profile
- **Filter by industry, location, CIDB grade, B-BBEE level**
- **Notify you instantly** (email, SMS, dashboard)
- **Give you time** to prepare quality proposals

**Real impact**: Construction contractor missing 2 tenders/month = R100K-R500K lost annually. With alerts = zero missed opportunities.

## How Tender Alerts Work

**Step 1: Set preferences**
- Industry/category
- Geographic areas (provinces)
- Budget range
- Certifications (CIDB, B-BBEE)
- Notification method

**Step 2: System scans daily**
- Monitors eTenders, departments, municipalities, SOEs
- Identifies matching tenders
- Filters irrelevant opportunities

**Step 3: You get notified**

Email example:
```
Subject: New Tender Match: Road Maintenance [Gauteng, R2.5M]

Tender: Road Resurfacing Project
Buyer: Ekurhuleni Municipality
Budget: R2.5M
Your Match: 85% (CIDB Grade 5 required, you have Grade 5)
Closing: 20 September 2026 (17 days left)
```

**Step 4: You bid**
- Review RFQ (you have 2-3 weeks, not 2-3 days)
- Prepare quality proposal
- Gather documents
- Submit before deadline

## Types of Tenders You'll See

- **Goods**: IT equipment, supplies, materials
- **Services**: Professional services, maintenance, logistics, IT support
- **Construction**: Road work, building, electrical, HVAC
- **Hybrid**: Supply + installation, supply + maintenance

## Customize Your Alerts

**Industry**: Construction, IT, Cleaning, Transport, Manufacturing, Professional Services, etc.

**Geography**: National, by province, by municipality

**Budget**: Under R500K, R500K-R5M, R5M-R50M, R50M+

**Qualifications**: System auto-filters by your CIDB, B-BBEE, CSD status

**Example**: You're CIDB Grade 3, B-BBEE Level 4
- ✓ Shows: Tenders requiring Grade 1-3
- ✗ Hides: Tenders requiring Grade 5+ (you can't bid)

## Notification Frequency

- **Daily digest**: All matches sent 9am
- **Real-time**: Instant alerts (can be noisy)
- **Weekly**: All tenders posted that week

**Recommendation**: Start with daily digest, adjust based on volume.

**Typical volume**:
- Construction (Johannesburg): 15-25 alerts/week
- IT Services (National): 8-15/week
- Cleaning (Local): 3-8/week

## Best Practices

1. **Set realistic filters** — Too broad = overwhelming. Right size = 8-15/week.
2. **Act quickly** — Start on proposal same day, don't wait.
3. **Track submissions** — Record tender name, bid amount, result.
4. **Update as you grow** — When you upgrade CIDB or B-BBEE, expand alert criteria.
5. **Filter strategically** — Don't bid on everything. Focus on profitable opportunities.

## Getting Started

**Setup (5 minutes)**:
1. Log in to supplier profile
2. Enable "Tender Alerts"
3. Set industry, location, budget, certifications
4. Choose notification method
5. Save

**First week**: Review sample alerts, refine filters until you're getting 8-15/week.

**Ongoing**: Check alerts daily, bid on strategic opportunities.

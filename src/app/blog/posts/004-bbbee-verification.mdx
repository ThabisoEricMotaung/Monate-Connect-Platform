# B-BBEE Verification: Levels Explained & How to Improve

**B-BBEE** = **Broad-Based Black Economic Empowerment**

It's South Africa's policy to measure and promote black ownership, management, and participation. Government uses B-BBEE as a procurement criteria—your level directly impacts your ability to win tenders.

Most government tenders require **B-BBEE Level 3 or better**. Level 1-2 = competitive advantage. Level 8 = essentially shut out.

## B-BBEE Levels 1-8

| Level | Black Ownership | Government Preference |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | 95%+ | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Highest priority |
| **2** | 75-95% | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ High priority |
| **3** | 50-75% | ⭐⭐⭐ Competitive |
| **4** | 25-50% | ⭐⭐ Less preferred |
| **5-7** | <25% | ⭐ Very limited |
| **8** | Non-compliant | Ineligible |

**Key insight**: Level 1 companies get substantial scoring advantage. Level 3 is minimum for serious government business.

## What Goes Into Your Score

B-BBEE calculated across 5 pillars:

1. **Ownership** (25 pts) — % black ownership, economic interest
2. **Management** (10 pts) — Board & executive black composition
3. **Employment Equity** (10 pts) — Black staff at all levels, women in management
4. **Procurement** (15 pts) — % spend from B-BBEE suppliers
5. **Skills Development** (10 pts) — Training investment, bursaries

**Bonuses**: Enterprise development (5 pts), Socio-economic development (5 pts)

**Total**: 100 points → Level 1 (90+ points)

## How to Get B-BBEE Certified

1. **Find SANAS-accredited verification agent** (Deloitte, KPMG, specialist firms)
2. **Gather documents**: Financials, employment records, procurement records, training docs
3. **Agent conducts assessment** (3-6 weeks)
4. **Receive certificate** (valid 3 years)

**Cost**: R8K-R25K depending on company size

## Quick Wins to Improve Your Level

- **Procurement** (3-5 pts): Switch 20% suppliers to B-BBEE Level 1-3
- **Training** (2-3 pts): Spend 1-2% payroll on employee training
- **Management** (2-4 pts): Promote black employees to supervisory roles
- **Ownership** (10+ pts): Bring in black shareholder/partner (biggest lever)

## Maintaining Your Level

Annual compliance:
- ✓ Pay SARS taxes on time
- ✓ Maintain black staff %
- ✓ Track procurement from B-BBEE suppliers
- ✓ Record training investments
- ✓ Board composition aligned

Re-certify every 3 years.

## B-BBEE in Government Tenders

Example tender scoring:
- Price: 60 points
- B-BBEE: 20 points
- Experience: 20 points

**B-BBEE points awarded**:
- Level 1: 20/20
- Level 2: 18/20
- Level 3: 15/20
- Level 4: 12/20
- Level 5+: 8/20

Level 1 supplier needs only 5% lower price to match Level 5 on total score.

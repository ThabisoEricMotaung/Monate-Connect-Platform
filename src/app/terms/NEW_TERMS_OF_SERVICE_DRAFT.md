## 4. SUPPLIER VERIFICATION AND SMARTSCORE

### 4.1 What "Verified" Means

**Verification Definition:**  
"Verified" on AiForm means that AiForm has reviewed specific documentary evidence on a specific date and confirmed it matches stated criteria.

**Example:**  
A supplier's CSD verification means:
- AiForm reviewed the CSD registration report dated [date]
- AiForm confirmed the registration number matches the supplier's profile
- AiForm confirmed the entity name matches the CSD record
- AiForm confirmed CSD status was "Active" as of that date

### 4.2 What Verification Does NOT Mean

Verification does **NOT** mean:
- The supplier is recommended, approved, or endorsed by AiForm
- The supplier is honest, trustworthy, or ethical
- The information remains current or accurate today
- The supplier can perform work or deliver goods successfully
- The supplier is solvent, capable, or in good financial standing
- The supplier complies with all applicable laws or regulations
- The supplier is suitable for your specific procurement needs
- The supplier has not engaged in fraud or misconduct
- AiForm stands behind or guarantees the supplier

### 4.3 Expiry and Change
- Verified documents have expiry dates (CSD, BBBEE, tax clearance, CIDB, etc.)
- AiForm does not track whether a supplier has renewed expired documents
- A previously verified supplier may no longer meet requirements
- You must independently verify current compliance status before contracting

### 4.4 Verification Methods
- **AiForm Reviewed:** AiForm examined the document and confirmed it matches the supplier's claims
- **AiForm Confirmed:** AiForm verified directly with the issuing authority (SARS TCS PIN, CIPC, CSD live lookup) — highest confidence
- **Supplier Provided:** Supplier uploaded the document; AiForm has not yet reviewed it

### 4.5 SmartScore Explained

**What SmartScore Is:**
SmartScore is an algorithmic indicator combining:
- **Compliance Points (up to 60 points):**
  - Business profile registration: 20 points
  - CSD registration: 20 points
  - B-BBEE certificate: 10 or 20 points
  - SARS tax compliance: 15 points
  - Banking verification: 10 points
  - Director verification: 10 points (optional)
  - Company profile document: 5 points (optional)
- **Activity Bonus (up to 8 points):**
  - Quotes submitted, contracts completed, ratings received
- **Total Score Range: 0–100 points**

**Score Bands:**
- Emerging: 0–39 points
- Developing: 40–59 points
- Reliable: 60–74 points
- Trusted: 75–84 points
- Elite: 85–100 points

### 4.6 What SmartScore Does NOT Mean

SmartScore is **NOT**:
- A credit score or financial rating
- A compliance certificate issued by any authority
- A guarantee of supplier performance or capability
- A guarantee of supplier honesty or integrity
- A recommendation to award a contract
- A substitute for your due diligence
- Predictive of future performance or payment behaviour
- Audited, certified, or endorsed by third parties
- A measure of past project quality or customer satisfaction

### 4.7 What Affects and Does NOT Affect SmartScore

**DOES Affect:**
- Expiry of verified compliance documents (score drops if not renewed)
- Completion of platform activities (quotes, contracts, ratings)
- AiForm's correction of erroneous underlying data
- Changes to AiForm's scoring algorithm

**Does NOT Affect:**
- Supplier's actual payment history or financial standing
- References or past project feedback
- Litigation, complaints, or regulatory action against the supplier
- Work quality or customer satisfaction
- Compliance with laws outside AiForm's visibility
- Negative events not visible on the Platform

### 4.8 Score Changes
- SmartScore may change without notice as underlying documents expire or are renewed
- AiForm may correct erroneous scores if underlying information is discovered to be inaccurate
- You will not receive notice of score changes
- You are responsible for monitoring supplier compliance

### 4.9 Challenging SmartScore
If you believe your SmartScore is inaccurate:
1. Identify the underlying information that is incorrect (e.g., document date, verification status)
2. Email support@aiformstudio.com with:
   - Your supplier ID
   - The incorrect information
   - Evidence of correct information
3. AiForm will review within 10 business days and correct if warranted

### 4.10 Buyer Responsibility
Before awarding a contract to any supplier:
1. Do NOT rely solely on SmartScore

   - Check SARS TCS status directly at My Compliance Profile

## 5. COMPLIANCE PASSPORT: VERIFICATION STATES

### 5.1 Three Verification States
AiForm displays three states for compliance documents:

**1. Supplier Provided**
- Supplier uploaded the document
- AiForm has not reviewed it
- Status is NOT verified; it is a supplier claim only
- Confidence level: LOW

**2. Reviewed by AiForm**
- AiForm examined the document
- AiForm confirmed it matches the supplier's stated details (name, date, reference number, etc.)
- Document appeared authentic and readable
- Limited to format and basic matching; not a regulatory audit
- Confidence level: MEDIUM
- Still requires independent verification by buyer

**3. Confirmed from Official Source**
- AiForm verified the document directly with the issuing authority
- E.g., SARS TCS PIN lookup, CIPC direct search, CSD live check, CIDB database query
- Reflects current status as of verification date
- Highest confidence in authenticity and currency
- Confidence level: HIGH
- Still requires independent re-verification by buyer before award

### 5.2 Limitations of All States
- Reviewed and confirmed documents may still be fraudulent (beyond inspection detection)
- Confirmed documents may be outdated if authority records are stale
- Confirmation does not guarantee supplier eligibility for your specific procurement
- All three states require independent buyer verification before contract award

# How to Respond to Opportunities on AiForm Procure

**Page Path:** `/how-to/respond-to-opportunities`
**Purpose:** Step-by-step guide for suppliers to find and respond to procurement opportunities
**Word Count:** ~750 words
**Schema:** HowTo (see Step 3)

---

## Overview

Responding to opportunities on AiForm Procure is how you win procurement work. Whether it's government tenders, private RFQs, or buyer requests, this guide walks you through finding the right opportunities and submitting a winning quote.

---

## What You'll Need

**Before Responding:**
- Completed supplier profile with verification badges (improves visibility)
- Relevant compliance documents (CSD, B-BBEE, tax—as required by the opportunity)
- Pricing and delivery capability information
- Contact details for the buyer if needed

---

## Step 1: Log In to AiForm

Visit **https://www.aiformprocure.co.za** and click **"Log In"** (top-right).

Enter your email and password. Click **"Sign In"**.

You'll see your **Supplier Dashboard**.

---

## Step 2: Find Opportunities

Click **"Opportunities"** in the main menu.

You'll see a feed of **Live Opportunities** matching your profile. Each shows:
- Title (what they're buying)
- Issuing authority or buyer
- Closing date (red if closing soon)
- Location and category
- Your match score (if matched automatically)

---

## Step 3: Filter Opportunities (Optional)

Refine your search to find the right opportunities:

**By Location:**
Click **"Location"** → Select your province(s). Example: "Gauteng", "Western Cape".

**By Category:**
Click **"Category"** → Select what you offer. Example: "Construction", "IT Services", "Goods".

**By Status:**
Click **"Status"** → "Open" (default) or "Closing Soon" (within 1 week).

**By Budget (if shown):**
Click **"Budget"** → Set a range. Example: "R50K–R500K".

**Search:**
Use the search box to find specific tenders. Example: "office furniture", "construction materials".

---

## Step 4: Read the Opportunity Details

Click on an opportunity that interests you.

You'll see:

**Summary:**
- Title and brief description
- Issuing authority or buyer
- Closing date and time (count-down timer)
- Estimated value (if disclosed)
- Location

**Requirements:**
- What they're buying (detailed specifications)
- Compliance documents required (CSD, B-BBEE, tax, etc.)
- Timeline and delivery location
- Special conditions or preferences

**Documents:**
- Download the tender brief, specification sheet, or attachment(s)
- Read carefully—this is the full procurement requirements

**Verification Badges:**
- See if you meet the compliance requirements
- If you're missing a required document, you won't be able to respond until you upload it

---

## Step 5: Check If You Qualify

**Do you meet the requirements?**

- ☑ I have all required compliance documents (verified ✓)
- ☑ I can deliver to the specified location
- ☑ I can meet the deadline
- ☑ I can deliver the specified quality
- ☑ My pricing is competitive

**If you answer no to any:**
- For missing documents: Go to your profile → Compliance Documents → Upload missing document
- For delivery location: Contact the issuing authority to see if your location qualifies
- For timeline: Contact the issuing authority or buyer to ask for an extension
- For quality/capability: Only respond if you're confident you can deliver

---

## Step 6: Prepare Your Response

**What to Include:**

**1. Quotation / Price:**
- Total price (all-inclusive if possible)
- Price breakdown (if required)
- Currency (ZAR)
- Validity period (usually 30 days)
- Payment terms (e.g., "30% on order, 70% on delivery")

**2. Delivery Details:**
- Delivery timeline (e.g., "2 weeks from order")
- Delivery location (confirm you can deliver there)
- Shipping method (courier, your vehicle, buyer's pickup)
- Any delivery conditions

**3. Compliance Statement:**
- Confirm you have all required documents verified on AiForm
- Example: "Our company is CSD registered, B-BBEE Level 2, and SARS compliant. All documents verified on AiForm."

**4. Capability Statement (Optional):**
- Why you're the right choice
- Relevant past projects
- Team expertise
- Why you'll deliver on time and on budget

**5. Attachments (if required):**
- Proforma invoice
- Compliance documents (if not already verified on AiForm)
- Technical specification sheet
- References or past project examples

---

## Step 7: Submit Your Response

On the opportunity detail page, click **"Respond"** or **"Submit Quote"**.

**Submission Form:**

**Quote Amount:**
Enter your total price (ZAR).

**Quote Details:**
Paste or type your quote information (price breakdown, delivery timeline, payment terms, capability statement).

**Attachments:**
Upload any required documents (proforma invoice, specifications, etc.).

**Confirmation:**
Check the box confirming accuracy of information.

Click **"Submit Quote"**.

Your response is now submitted! ✅

---

## Step 8: Track Your Response

Go to **"My Responses"** in your dashboard.

You'll see:

**Pending Quotes:**
- Opportunity name, closing date (countdown)
- Your quote amount
- Status: "Submitted" or "Under Review"

**Awarded Contracts:**
- Opportunities where your quote was accepted
- Click to view contract details, delivery timeline, contact info

**Unsuccessful Bids:**
- Opportunities where you weren't selected
- You can still contact the buyer for feedback

**Message with Buyer:**
- Click "Message" to ask questions or negotiate
- Keep all communication on AiForm for transparency

## Best Practices

**Submit Early:**
- Don't wait until the last day
- Closing deadlines are strict
- Early submission shows professionalism

**Read Requirements Carefully:**
- Don't skip the tender documents
- Missing a requirement can result in rejection
- Ask for clarification if confused

**Competitive Pricing:**
- Research the market
- Quote competitively but not so low you lose money
- Explain your value proposition

**Clear Communication:**
- Make your quote easy to read
- Include all required information
- Be professional and specific

**Follow Instructions:**
- Attach documents if required
- Use requested formats
- Respond to all questions asked

**Update Your Profile:**
- Keep compliance documents current
- Update your availability and service areas
- Respond promptly to buyer messages

## Troubleshooting

**"I don't see the opportunity I'm looking for":**
- Search by keyword (e.g., "construction", "supplies")
- Check your location filter—expand to all provinces if needed
- Check the closing date; past-date opportunities are archived

**"I'm not qualified for the opportunity":**
- You may be missing a required compliance document
- Go to your profile and upload the missing document
- Contact support@aiformstudio.com if you believe you qualify but aren't shown

**"My quote was rejected":**
- You may not have met compliance requirements
- Your price may have been too high
- Contact the buyer (via Message) to ask for feedback

**"I didn't get a response from the buyer":**
- Buyers may be evaluating multiple quotes
- Reaching out via Message can help get a response
- If no response after closing date, they may have selected another supplier

# How to Register as a Supplier on AiForm Procure

**Page Path:** `/how-to/register-supplier`
**Purpose:** Step-by-step guide to supplier registration, document requirements, and profile setup
**Word Count:** ~800 words
**Schema:** HowTo (see Step 3)

---

## Overview

Registering as a supplier on AiForm Procure is free and takes about 10–15 minutes. Once registered, you can search government tenders, post RFQs to buy, upload compliance documents, and be discovered by buyers looking for verified suppliers.

This guide walks you through each step.

---

## What You'll Need to Register

**Required:**
- Email address (verified during registration)
- Phone number
- Company name (must match CIPC registration)
- Company registration number (CIPC)
- Password (at least 8 characters, mix of letters/numbers/symbols)

**Optional (but improves your profile):**
- Company logo
- Company description
- Services you offer
- Operating provinces/regions
- Website URL

**Document upload (after registration):**
- CSD registration report
- B-BBEE certificate or sworn affidavit
- SARS tax clearance or TCS PIN
- Bank letter
- Other compliance documents

---

## Step 1: Go to AiForm Procure

Visit **https://www.aiformprocure.co.za**

Click the **"Register as Supplier"** button (or **"Sign Up"** in the top-right menu).

---

## Step 2: Create Your Account

Fill in the registration form:

**Email Address:** Use your business email if possible. You'll receive a verification email—click the link to confirm your email.

**Phone Number:** A real, working number. AiForm may use this for verification or support.

**First Name & Last Name:** Your name (will appear on your profile initially; you can update later).

**Company Name:** Must match your CIPC registration. Examples: "ABC Supplies (Pty) Ltd", "XYZ Trading".

**Company Registration Number:** Your CIPC registration number (format: CCXXXXXXXXXX or XXXXXXXXXX). This is used to verify your company exists.

**Password:** At least 8 characters, with a mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols. Example: "MyCompany2026!Secure".

**Confirm Password:** Re-enter your password.

**Terms of Service & Privacy:** Read and check both boxes. These explain your rights and responsibilities on AiForm.

Click **"Create Account"**.

---

## Step 3: Verify Your Email

You'll see a confirmation page: **"Check your email to verify your account."**

Check your email inbox (and spam folder) for a message from AiForm. Click the **"Verify Email"** link.

You're now registered!

---

## Step 4: Complete Your Supplier Profile

After email verification, log in to AiForm. You'll see your **Supplier Dashboard**.

Click **"Complete Your Profile"** or go to **Profile Settings**.

**Fill in the following (optional but recommended):**

**Company Details:**
- Company description (50–200 words about what you do)
- Industry/categories (select all that apply: e.g., "Construction", "Professional Services", "Goods Supply")
- Operating provinces (where you operate: e.g., Gauteng, Western Cape)
- Website URL (if you have one)
- Company logo (upload your logo; improves visibility)

**Contact Information:**
- Primary contact person (name and title)
- Email (can be different from your login email)
- Phone number
- Office address

**Services Offered:**
- List 3–5 main services or products (buyers search by these)

Click **"Save Profile"**.

---

## Step 5: Upload Compliance Documents

Your profile is now live, but you have **0 compliance points**. To improve your **SmartScore** and be eligible for most procurements, you need to upload documents.

Go to **Profile → Compliance Documents**.

Click **"Upload Document"** for each of the following:

### CSD Registration (Priority 1)
1. Get your CSD registration report from https://www.csd.gov.za (free)
2. Click "CSD Registration"
3. Upload the PDF or image
4. AiForm will verify within 2–5 business days

### SARS Tax Compliance (Priority 1)
1. Get your SARS TCS PIN or tax clearance certificate from https://www.sars.gov.za
2. Click "Tax Compliance"
3. Upload the document
4. AiForm will verify within 2–5 business days

### Banking Verification (Priority 1)
1. Request a bank letter from your bank confirming:
   - Account holder name (must match your company name)
   - Account number
   - Branch code
   - Account type
2. Click "Banking Details"
3. Upload the bank letter
4. AiForm will verify within 1–3 business days

### B-BBEE Certificate (Priority 2)
1. Obtain your B-BBEE verification certificate from an accredited agency, or a sworn affidavit if you're an EME
2. Click "B-BBEE Certificate"
3. Upload the document
4. AiForm will verify within 2–5 business days

### Other Documents (Optional)
- CIDB grading (if construction-related)
- COIDA letter (if labour-intensive)
- Professional qualifications
- Insurance certificates
- Company profile/capability statement

---

## Step 6: Wait for Verification

AiForm staff will review each document within 5 business days.

**You'll receive an email when:**
- Document is verified ✅
- Document is rejected ❌ (with reason and how to fix)

Once documents are verified, your **SmartScore** will increase and your profile will show verification badges.

---

## Step 7: Start Finding Opportunities

Once you're registered and verified, you can:

1. **Search Opportunities:** Go to **Opportunities** → filter by location, category, closing date
2. **Get Matched:** AiForm automatically recommends opportunities based on your profile
3. **Respond to RFQs:** Click "Respond" or "Submit Quote" on opportunities you're interested in
4. **Track Your Responses:** Go to **My Responses** to see all your pending and completed quotes

## Improving Your Profile After Registration

**To increase your SmartScore:**
- Upload and verify all compliance documents
- Add a company logo and description
- Complete all optional fields (website, phone, address)
- Keep documents renewed before they expire
- Submit quotes and complete contracts to build activity points

**To be discovered by buyers:**
- Use keywords in your company description (e.g., "construction", "supplies", "professional services")
- Select your main industries and service categories
- List all operating provinces where you work
- Keep your contact information current

## Troubleshooting

**"My company wasn't found" when registering:**
- Ensure your company is registered with CIPC and your name matches exactly
- You may not be the authorised person on the CIPC registration—contact your co-director

**"Email verification didn't arrive":**
- Check your spam/junk folder
- Wait 5 minutes, then click "Resend verification email"
- Contact support@aiformstudio.com if it still doesn't arrive

**"My document was rejected":**
- Read the rejection reason carefully
- Upload a clearer scan or corrected document
- Ensure all details match your profile exactly
- Resubmit

**Questions about CSD/B-BBEE/Tax registration:**
- Visit the relevant guide: `/guides/csd`, `/guides/bbbee`, `/guides/tax-compliance-status`
- Contact the relevant authority directly (CSD, SARS, your accountant)

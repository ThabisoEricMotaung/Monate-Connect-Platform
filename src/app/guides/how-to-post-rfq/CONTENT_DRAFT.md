# How to Post an RFQ on AiForm Procure

**Page Path:** `/how-to/post-rfq`
**Purpose:** Step-by-step guide for buyers to create and post procurement RFQs
**Word Count:** ~750 words
**Schema:** HowTo (see Step 3)

---

## Overview

As a buyer, posting an RFQ (Request for Quote) on AiForm Procure connects you with verified suppliers in seconds. Your RFQ is matched to suppliers automatically, and they can submit quotes directly on the platform.

This guide walks you through posting your first RFQ.

---

## What You'll Need

**Required:**
- AiForm buyer account (email and password)
- Procurement details (what you're buying, budget, deadline)
- At least 3 days before your preferred closing date

**Optional:**
- Detailed specifications document (attach as PDF)
- Drawings, images, or technical requirements
- Reference materials or examples

---

## Step 1: Log In to AiForm

Visit **https://www.aiformprocure.co.za** and click **"Log In"** (top-right).

Enter your email and password. Click **"Sign In"**.

You'll be taken to your **Buyer Dashboard**.

---

## Step 2: Start a New RFQ

Click the **"Create RFQ"** button (top of dashboard).

Or go to **Procurement → New RFQ**.

You'll see the **New RFQ Form**.

---

## Step 3: Fill in Basic Details

**RFQ Title:**
What are you buying? Be specific.
- ✅ Good: "Office furniture—2 x desks, 4 x chairs, 1 x cabinet"
- ❌ Vague: "Furniture"

**RFQ Reference (Optional):**
Internal reference number. Example: "REF-2026-001", "PROJECT-XYZ-001". If you leave blank, AiForm generates one.

**Description:**
2–5 sentence summary of what you need and why. Example:
"We need office furniture for our Johannesburg office renovation. Requirements: 2 computer desks, 4 office chairs (ergonomic), 1 filing cabinet. Project timeline: delivery by end of August. We're open to brands and materials as long as quality is high and delivery is on-time."

**Category:**
Select the primary category (e.g., "Furniture", "Construction", "IT Services"). Suppliers search by category.

**Industry:**
Optional. Select your industry (e.g., "Technology", "Finance", "Non-Profit"). Helps with matching.

Click **"Next"**.

---

## Step 4: Set Budget and Timeline

**Budget (Optional):**
Your estimated budget range. Example: "R50,000 – R100,000".
- Leave blank if you want supplier quotes first
- Suppliers can filter by budget range, so including this helps matching

**Currency:**
ZAR (South African Rand) is the default.

**Closing Date & Time:**
When suppliers must submit quotes by. Example: "30 August 2026, 5:00 PM".
- Recommend at least 5–7 business days for complex RFQs
- AiForm will notify matched suppliers automatically
- Suppliers receive reminders 3 days before closing

**Delivery Location:**
Where the goods/services will be delivered. Example: "Johannesburg, Gauteng". Used for location-based matching.

**Delivery Timeline:**
When do you need it? Example: "2 weeks from order", "30 September 2026".

Click **"Next"**.

---

## Step 5: Add Specifications and Documents

**Detailed Specifications:**
Copy-paste your detailed requirements here (or leave blank if simple RFQ).

Example:
```
Office Furniture Specifications:
- 2x Computer desks: minimum 1.2m x 0.6m, laminate or wood finish
- 4x Office chairs: ergonomic, leather or fabric, with adjustable height
- 1x Filing cabinet: 4-drawer, lockable, metal or wood
- Delivery: Johannesburg CBD, ground floor
- Installation: Supplier to position furniture
- Timeline: 2 weeks from order confirmation
```

**Attachments:**
Upload documents (PDF, Word, Images, Excel):
- Technical drawings
- Specification sheets
- Reference images
- Compliance requirements
- Previous RFQs (if similar)

Click **"Upload File"** for each document.

Click **"Next"**.

---

## Step 6: Set Compliance Requirements

**Do suppliers need specific compliance?**

Check any that apply:
- ☐ CSD Registration (government preference)
- ☐ B-BBEE Certification (government preference)
- ☐ Tax Compliance (SARS verification)
- ☐ Banking Verification
- ☐ CIDB Grading (construction only)
- ☐ Insurance (if applicable)
- ☐ Professional Qualifications (if applicable)

AiForm will only show your RFQ to suppliers who meet your requirements.

Leave unchecked if you're open to all suppliers regardless of compliance.

Click **"Next"**.

---

## Step 7: Review and Post

Review your RFQ details:
- Title, description, category
- Budget, timeline, delivery location
- Specifications and attachments
- Compliance requirements

**Edit:** Click back if you need to change anything.

**Terms of Service:** Check the box confirming you've read and agreed to terms.

Click **"Post RFQ"**.

Your RFQ is now live! ✅

---

## Step 8: Manage Responses

You'll see your **RFQ Dashboard** with:

**Matched Suppliers:** AiForm shows how many verified suppliers match your requirements.

**Quotes Received:** Suppliers can submit quotes starting immediately. You'll receive email notifications.

**Message Suppliers:** Click "Message" to ask clarification questions or negotiate.

**Track Quotes:** See which suppliers have quoted, closing date countdown, and quote comparison.

**Award Contract:** Once you've chosen a supplier, click "Award" to formally accept their quote.

## Best Practices

**Clear Specifications:**
- Be as detailed as possible (suppliers can't ask for clarifications you won't provide)
- Include drawings, images, or examples
- Specify quality standards and acceptance criteria

**Realistic Timeline:**
- 5–7 business days minimum for complex RFQs
- 2–3 business days for simple/urgent RFQs
- Factor in your internal approval time

**Compliance Requirements:**
- Only require what you actually need (unnecessary requirements limit responses)
- Specify why (e.g., "CIDB Grade 3+ required for construction safety")

**Two-Way Communication:**
- Monitor messages and respond to supplier questions
- Clarify ambiguous requirements early
- Negotiate terms if needed

**Document Everything:**
- Keep all quotes and correspondence on AiForm
- Award formally via the platform (creates audit trail)
- Attach final contract/PO to the award

## Troubleshooting

**"No suppliers are matching my RFQ":**
- Your compliance requirements may be too strict; consider relaxing them
- Your budget may be too low; consider raising it
- Your timeline may be too short; allow more days
- Your category may be too narrow; try a different industry

**"I got few responses":**
- Message suppliers directly asking if they're interested
- Consider extending the closing date
- Review and clarify specifications

**"Suppliers are quoting above budget":**
- Negotiate with the best quotes
- Revise your specification to reduce scope
- Re-post with a higher budget

## What Is SmartScore?

SmartScore is an algorithmic indicator on AiForm Procure that combines your compliance documentation, verification status, and platform activity into a single score from 0 to 100. It's designed to help buyers quickly assess your engagement and compliance with major requirements like CSD registration, B-BBEE certification, tax compliance, and banking verification.

Think of it as your "procurement readiness" score on AiForm—not a credit score, not a regulatory certification, but a measure of how completely you've documented and verified your compliance on the platform.

---

## How Is SmartScore Calculated?

SmartScore has two main components: **Compliance Points** and **Activity Bonus Points**.

### Compliance Points (Up to 100 Points Before the Overall Score Cap)

Your compliance score is based on specific verified documents:

- **Business Profile Registration (20 points):** Your company name, registration details, and industry information match official records.
- **CSD Registration (20 points):** Your Central Supplier Database (CSD) registration has been verified and is current.
- **B-BBEE Certificate (10 or 20 points):** You have submitted a current B-BBEE verification certificate or sworn affidavit. Verified Levels 1–4 earn 20 points and verified Levels 5–8 earn 10 points.
- **SARS Tax Compliance (15 points):** Your SARS Tax Compliance Status (TCS) PIN or tax clearance certificate has been verified.
- **Banking Verification (10 points):** You have provided a valid bank letter confirming your account details.
- **Director Verification (10 points, optional):** Director identity information has been verified against CIPC records.
- **Company Profile Document (5 points, optional):** You have uploaded a company profile or capability statement.

### Activity Bonus Points (Up to 8 Points)

You earn bonus points for platform activity:

- **Quotes Submitted:** Each quote you submit adds activity points.
- **Contracts Completed:** Finished contracts add bonus points.
- **Ratings Received:** Positive feedback from buyers increases activity points.

The activity bonus is capped at 8 points. Recent activity and profile updates are evaluated using the platform's current scoring windows, so older activity can contribute less over time.

### Total Score Range: 0–100

Your final SmartScore is the sum of verified compliance points plus activity bonus points, capped at 100.

- **0–39:** Emerging
- **40–59:** Developing
- **60–74:** Reliable
- **75–84:** Trusted
- **85–100:** Elite

## Why Did My SmartScore Change?

Your SmartScore can change for several reasons:

**Document Expiry:** If your CSD registration, B-BBEE certificate, or tax clearance expires, you lose the compliance points associated with that document. Your score drops until you renew and re-verify.

**Renewed Documents:** When you upload a renewed document (new B-BBEE certificate, fresh tax clearance), AiForm re-verifies it. Your score increases once verification is complete.

**Activity Decay:** If you haven't submitted quotes or completed contracts recently, your activity bonus points may decrease over time.

**AiForm Corrections:** If AiForm discovers erroneous information (a mismatched document, duplicate verification), it may correct your score. You'll be notified of corrections.

**Algorithm Updates:** AiForm may adjust the scoring algorithm to better reflect procurement readiness. Changes are announced in advance.

## Can I Improve My SmartScore?

Yes. Here are the most direct ways:

**1. Complete Missing Compliance Documents**
- If you don't have CSD (20 pts), register at https://www.csd.gov.za
- If you don't have B-BBEE (10–20 pts), submit a certificate or sworn affidavit
- If you don't have banking verification (10 pts), request a letter from your bank
- If you don't have tax compliance (15 pts), check your SARS TCS status

**2. Renew Expiring Documents**
- Check your profile for documents showing expiry dates
- Renew before they expire to avoid score drops
- Re-upload the renewed document to AiForm for re-verification

**3. Be Active on the Platform**
- Submit quotes on opportunities you're interested in
- Complete contracts and ask buyers for ratings
- Activity bonus points reward engagement (up to 8 points)

**4. Upload Optional Documents**
- Add a company profile or capability statement (+5 points)
- Verify directors against CIPC if eligible (+10 points)

**5. Keep Information Current**
- Update your profile with current company details
- Refresh documents before they expire
- Respond promptly to verification requests

## Is SmartScore a Credit Score?

**No.** SmartScore is not a credit score in any way. It doesn't measure:

- Your payment history or creditworthiness
- Whether you pay bills on time
- Your financial stability or revenue
- Your ability to complete work
- Your honesty or reliability

SmartScore only measures how completely you've documented and verified your compliance on AiForm. It's a **procurement readiness indicator**, not a financial rating.

---

## Is SmartScore a Regulatory Certification?

**No.** SmartScore is not issued or approved by any government agency or regulatory body. It's an AiForm-created indicator based on documents you upload and information we verify.

SmartScore is not:
- A B-BBEE status level (that comes from your actual B-BBEE certificate)
- A SARS compliance certificate (that comes from SARS)
- A CSD status confirmation (that comes from the CSD)
- A guarantee of compliance with any law

Buyers should never rely solely on SmartScore to assess your compliance. They should verify your actual documents and status with official authorities.

## Can I Dispute My SmartScore?

Yes. If you believe your SmartScore is inaccurate, you can request a review.

**How to Request a Review:**

1. Go to your supplier profile
2. Click "SmartScore" badge → "Request Review"
3. Explain which information you believe is incorrect
4. Provide evidence (e.g., a new document, official verification)

**AiForm will review within 10 business days** and correct your score if the underlying information is inaccurate.

**Common Reasons for Disputes:**

- A document was rejected but you've since uploaded a corrected version
- Your profile shows expired information that you've actually renewed
- Verification failed due to a mismatch you've now resolved

---

## What Does SmartScore NOT Guarantee?

SmartScore does **not** guarantee:

- Your capability to perform work
- Your honesty or integrity
- Your solvency or financial stability
- Your past project quality
- That you comply with all laws
- That you're suitable for a specific procurement
- That you won't commit fraud

**SmartScore is informational only.** Buyers remain responsible for conducting independent due diligence before contracting.

---

## Can My SmartScore Be Corrected?

Yes. If AiForm discovers that underlying information is inaccurate (a mismatched document, duplicate verification, or fraudulent data), it may correct your SmartScore.

**You'll be notified via email** of any corrections, along with an explanation.

If you disagree with a correction, you can request a review using the "Request Review" button on your SmartScore badge.

---

## How Often Is SmartScore Updated?

SmartScore is recalculated when the platform refreshes your canonical supplier score, based on:

- Document expiry dates (documents expire at the end of the day they're due)
- Recent platform activity (quotes submitted, contracts completed)
- Renewed documents that have been re-verified

Verified document changes may not appear until the review and score refresh have completed.

---

## Who Can See My SmartScore?

Your SmartScore is **public** and visible to:

- Buyers searching the supplier directory
- Buyers viewing your profile
- Other suppliers searching for collaborators
- Anyone viewing AiForm's public supplier directory

Your SmartScore is displayed with a label (Emerging, Developing, Reliable, Trusted, Elite) and the numeric score.

## What Does "Verified" Mean on AiForm?

On AiForm Procure, **"Verified"** means that AiForm has reviewed specific documentary evidence on a specific date and confirmed it matches the information you provided.

**Example:** If your CSD registration shows a "Verified" badge, it means AiForm reviewed your CSD report on [date], confirmed your registration number matches your profile, confirmed your company name matches CSD records, and confirmed CSD status was "Active" as of that date.

### What "Verified" Does NOT Mean

**Verification does NOT mean:**
- Your supplier is recommended, approved, or endorsed by AiForm
- The information remains accurate or current today
- You're honest, trustworthy, or reliable
- You can complete work successfully
- You're suitable for a specific procurement
- AiForm stands behind you or guarantees you

Verification is point-in-time confirmation of document authenticity, nothing more.

## What Are the Three Verification States?

AiForm displays three different states for compliance documents:

### 1. Supplier Provided
- **What it means:** You uploaded the document, but AiForm hasn't reviewed it yet.
- **Confidence level:** LOW — it's your claim only.
- **Next step:** Wait for AiForm to review, or provide a clearer copy if the upload failed.

### 2. Reviewed by AiForm
- **What it means:** AiForm examined the document, confirmed it matches your stated details (name, date, reference number), and the document appeared authentic.
- **Confidence level:** MEDIUM — we checked format and basic matching, but didn't verify with the issuing authority.
- **Verification scope:** The document appears genuine and matches what you claimed; no deeper audit was performed.

### 3. Confirmed from Official Source
- **What it means:** AiForm verified directly with the issuing authority.
- **Examples:** SARS TCS PIN lookup, CIPC direct search, CSD live check, CIDB database query.
- **Confidence level:** HIGH — confirmed against the official source as of verification date.
- **Limitation:** Official records may be stale; confirmation is accurate as of the verification date, not necessarily today.

## How Long Does Verification Take?

**AiForm-Reviewed Documents:** 1–5 business days after upload.

**Confirmed-from-Source Documents:** 2–10 business days (depends on authority responsiveness).

**What can delay verification:**
- Unclear or low-resolution scans
- Mismatched details (company name, registration number)
- Authority delays (SARS, CIPC, CSD may take time to respond)
- Missing required fields

**To speed up verification:**
- Upload high-quality scans
- Ensure all details match your profile exactly
- Contact support@aiformstudio.com if a document is pending longer than expected

## What If My Document Is Rejected?

If AiForm rejects your document, you'll receive a notification explaining why.

**Common rejection reasons:**
- Document is unclear (low resolution, blurry)
- Details don't match (company name mismatch, wrong registration number)
- Document is expired
- Document appears to be a copy or forgery

**How to fix it:**
1. Review the rejection reason
2. Obtain a clear, official copy from the issuing authority
3. Ensure all details match your profile exactly
4. Re-upload via your profile → Compliance Documents
5. AiForm will re-review

**Repeated rejections?** Contact support@aiformstudio.com for assistance.

## How Long Does a Verification Last?

**Verified documents have expiry dates** (unless they're permanent, like CIPC registration).

**Expiry and validity:**
- Use the expiry date printed on the document or confirmed by its issuing authority.
- AiForm currently applies expiry-aware status checks to CSD, B-BBEE, SARS tax-clearance, and CIDB records when an expiry date is available.
- Banking and company-registration evidence may still need replacement when details change, even where no formal expiry date is printed.
- Tender-specific rules can require a more recent document, so always check the opportunity itself.

**What happens when a document expires?**
- Your SmartScore drops (you lose the compliance points for that document)
- The verification badge changes to "Expired"
- Buyers can still see that the document was once verified, but it's no longer current

**To avoid expiry:**
- Note expiry dates in your profile
- Renew documents 1–2 months before expiry
- Re-upload renewed documents for re-verification

## Do I Need All Compliance Documents?

**No.** Different procurements require different documents.

**Always required (for most procurements):**
- CSD registration (government procurement preference)
- SARS tax compliance
- Banking verification

**Often required:**
- B-BBEE certificate (government procurement preference, supplier development)
- Company registration (CIPC)

**Sometimes required:**
- CIDB grading (construction-related procurements)
- COIDA letter (labour-intensive procurements)
- Professional qualifications (professional services)
- Insurance certificates (certain industries)

**Check the opportunity** to see what documents are required for a specific tender.

## Can I Challenge a Verification?

Yes. If you believe AiForm's verification is inaccurate, you can request a review.

**How to request a review:**

1. Go to your profile → Compliance Documents
2. Click the rejected/disputed document
3. Click "Request Review"
4. Explain what is inaccurate
5. Provide supporting evidence (e.g., official document, authority confirmation)

**AiForm will review within 10 business days** and correct the verification if the underlying information is inaccurate.

**Common reasons to challenge:**
- A document was marked "Expired" but you believe it's still current
- A document was rejected but you have a clearer copy
- Information shows a mismatch you've now resolved

## What If My Information Changes?

If your company details change (name, address, registration), you must update your profile.

**How to update:**
1. Go to your profile → Company Details
2. Update the changed information
3. Upload supporting documentation if required (e.g., CIPC amendment certificate if your company name changed)
4. AiForm will review changes within 5 business days

**Important:** Outdated information can lead to verification failures. Keep your profile current.

---

## Can My Verification Be Revoked?

Yes, if AiForm discovers the underlying information is inaccurate or fraudulent.

**Reasons verification might be revoked:**
- Document is discovered to be forged or fraudulent
- Underlying authority (SARS, CIPC, CSD) reports information is false
- Information was misrepresented on the document

**If verification is revoked:**
- You'll be notified via email
- The verification badge will be removed
- You can request a review if you believe the revocation is in error

## Who Can See My Verified Documents?

**Verified documents themselves:** Only AiForm staff can see full documents.

**Verification badges:** Public (visible to all buyers and users viewing your profile).

**Banks details:** NEVER public; only shown to payment processors if needed.

**The information AiForm confirms:** Buyers see only that you're "Verified" for CSD, B-BBEE, tax, etc.—not the actual document.

## What If I Don't Agree with the Verification Decision?

You can request a review using the "Request Review" button on your document. AiForm will:

1. Re-examine the original document
2. Contact the relevant authority for re-confirmation if needed
3. Respond within 10 business days with a decision

If you still disagree, you can escalate to support@aiformstudio.com.

---

## Expired vs. Not Yet Verified—What's the Difference?

**Expired:** The document was verified, but the expiry date has passed. You need to renew and re-submit.

**Not Yet Verified:** You uploaded the document, but AiForm hasn't reviewed it. Status is "Supplier Provided."

**Missing:** You haven't uploaded a document yet. Status shows "No document uploaded."

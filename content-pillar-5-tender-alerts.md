# Tender Alerts: Get Notified of RFQs Instantly & Never Miss a Bid

**Meta Description:** Set up tender alerts to get notified of government RFQs in your industry. Real-time notifications for opportunities matching your CIDB grade, B-BBEE level, and location.

---

## The Problem: How Tenders Close Without You Knowing

You've probably heard this before:

*"We bid on that tender, but the deadline closed before we heard about it."*

Or worse:

*"There was a contract worth R500K in our category last month, but by the time we found out, it was closed."*

This happens because:

1. **Government tenders are announced on multiple platforms** — eTenders, department websites, municipal sites, newspapers. There's no single source.
2. **Deadlines are tight** — Many government RFQs give only 2-3 weeks to submit. If you're searching manually, you miss opportunities.
3. **Tender announcements are easy to miss** — One-off posts on government sites, not publicized aggressively.

**The result**: Massive lost revenue. Competitors beat you because they saw the opportunity first.

---

## The Solution: Tender Alerts

A **tender alert** system solves this by:

- **Automatically scanning** government tender platforms daily
- **Filtering** tenders that match your industry, location, and qualifications
- **Notifying you instantly** (email, SMS, or platform dashboard) when a matching opportunity appears
- **Giving you time** to prepare and submit a quality bid

**Real numbers**: If you're in construction and miss just 2 tenders/month, you're likely leaving R100K-R500K on the table annually.

## How Tender Alerts Work

### Step 1: Set Your Preferences

When you activate alerts, you define:

- **Industry/category**: Construction, IT, logistics, cleaning, manufacturing, etc.
- **Geographic areas**: Which provinces/regions you serve
- **Budget range**: Project values you can handle (R100K-R1M, R1M-R10M, etc.)
- **Certifications**: Your CIDB grade, B-BBEE level (system filters relevant tenders)
- **Notification method**: Email, SMS, or app notification

### Step 2: System Scans Daily

The alert system:
- Monitors government tender platforms 24/7
- Scans eTenders, department websites, municipalities, SOEs
- Identifies tenders matching your profile
- Filters out irrelevant opportunities

### Step 3: You Get Notified

When a matching tender is found, you receive:

**Via Email**:
```
Subject: New Tender Match: Road Maintenance [Gauteng, R2.5M]

A tender matching your profile has been posted:
- Tender: Road Resurfacing Project
- Buyer: Ekurhuleni Municipality
- Location: Gauteng
- Budget: R2.5 Million
- Your Match: 85% (CIDB Grade 5 required, you have Grade 5; B-BBEE Level 3 accepted)
- Closing Date: 20 September 2026 (17 days left)

View Full Tender → [LINK]
```

**Via Dashboard**: Tender alert appears in your dashboard with full details.

### Step 4: You Bid

Armed with advance notice, you:
1. Review the RFQ (you have 2-3 weeks, not 2-3 days)
2. Prepare a quality proposal
3. Gather required documents (B-BBEE cert, references, insurance)
4. Submit before deadline

**Result**: Higher win rate because you're prepared.

### Budget Range

Set your capacity:
- Under R500K
- R500K - R5M
- R5M - R50M
- R50M+

### Qualification Filters

The system automatically matches:
- Your CIDB grade (construction) → Only shows tenders you can bid on
- Your B-BBEE level → Only shows tenders accepting your level
- Your CSD status → Verifies you're eligible

**Example**: You're CIDB Grade 3, B-BBEE Level 4

System will show:
- ✓ Tenders requiring Grade 1, 2, or 3
- ✓ Tenders accepting Level 1-8 (you qualify)
- ✗ Tenders requiring Grade 5+ (filters out — you can't bid)

## Notification Frequency & Timing

### How Often Do You Get Alerts?

Depends on your settings:

- **Daily digest**: All matching tenders posted that day, sent 9am
- **Real-time**: Instant notification when a tender is posted (can be noisy)

**Recommendation**: Start with daily digest, adjust based on volume.

## Tender Alert Best Practices

### 1. Set Realistic Alerts

❌ **Too broad**: "All construction tenders in South Africa" = 50+ alerts/week (overwhelming)  
✓ **Right size**: "Commercial building, Gauteng + Western Cape, R1M-R50M" = 8-12 alerts/week

### 2. Act Quickly on Alerts

Tenders with tight deadlines require fast action:
- Read alert immediately
- Check if you genuinely qualify
- Start on proposal same day (don't wait)

### 3. Track Your Submissions

Keep records:
- Tender name, date submitted
- Bid amount
- Result (won/lost)
- Why you lost (price, qualifications, other)

This tells you which tender sources are most profitable.

### 4. Update Alerts When You Evolve

If you get CIDB upgraded or B-BBEE improved:
- Update your alert settings
- You'll now see tenders you previously couldn't bid on

### 5. Don't Bid Indiscriminately

Getting 10 tender alerts doesn't mean bid on all 10.

Filter by:
- Profitability (will you make money?)
- Strategic fit (builds your portfolio?)
- Win probability (do you qualify?

## Getting Started with Tender Alerts

### Setup (5 Minutes):

1. Sign up or log in to your supplier profile
2. Go to "Alerts & Preferences"
3. Enable "Tender Alerts"
4. Set your industry, location, budget, certifications
5. Choose notification method (email, SMS, app)
6. Save settings

### First Week:

- You'll get sample alerts matching your criteria
- Review them — are they relevant?
- If too many: narrow filters
- If too few: broaden filters
- Refine until "Goldilocks" zone (8-15 alerts/week)

### Ongoing:

- Check alerts daily or as they come in
- For interesting tenders, start bid prep immediately
- Track which alerts → bid submissions → wins
- Adjust alerts based on profitability data
